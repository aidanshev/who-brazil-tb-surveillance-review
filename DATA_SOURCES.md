# Data sources and acquisition

Raw third-party datasets are not mirrored here unless redistribution and privacy are clearly appropriate. Download from the official source, record retrieval date/version/size/SHA-256, and do not assume a newer download matches the historical analysis input.

| Source | Official site | Public-repository treatment |
|---|---|---|
| WHO Tuberculosis Data | https://www.who.int/teams/global-tuberculosis-programme/data/ | WHO inputs; raw source data excluded. |
| WHO Global Tuberculosis Report 2025 | https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240116924 | WHO report/context source. |
| Brazil SINAN/DATASUS TB cases | https://datasus.saude.gov.br/acesso-a-informacao/casos-de-tuberculose-desde-2001-sinan/ | Brazil surveillance source; patient-level data excluded. |
| IBGE territorial meshes | https://www.ibge.gov.br/geociencias/organizacao-do-territorio/malhas-territoriais/15774-malhas.html | Geographic source for generated maps. |
